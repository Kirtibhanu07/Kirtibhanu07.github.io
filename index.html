FEATURED / TIME-SERIES ANALYSIS

Reading 115 years
of rainfall.

How much can historical rainfall tell us about the next year? I explored seasonal patterns, unusual years and the limits of different forecasting methods.

22forecast configurations
20held-out test years
PythonStatsmodelsPlotly
View the analysis +

The approach

Chronological validation and expanding-window backtesting, with MAE, RMSE and block-bootstrap uncertainty estimates.

What the results showed

ARIMA test RMSE was 95.8 mm, 24.2% below a previous-year baseline. Historical mean won validation, with no clear ARIMA advantage over that stronger baseline.
